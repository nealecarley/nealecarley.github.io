Meet the Artist

Hi! I'm Neale, and I'm 5 years old! I love creating art with my pencils, crayons, and imagination. My favorite things to draw are all kinds of transportation - boats that sail across the ocean, cars that zoom down the street, airplanes that fly high in the sky, and even watermelon boats that you can eat!

Every week, I create a new piece of art and imagine wonderful stories about what I've drawn. Sometimes my drawings are in black and white with just my pencil, and sometimes they're full of bright, happy colors!

I hope you enjoy looking at my artwork and reading the stories I imagine about each picture. Art is like magic - it can take you anywhere your imagination wants to go!
